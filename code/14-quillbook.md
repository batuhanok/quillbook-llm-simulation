# Quillbook: client dossier

Fictional company. Sector: B2B software (cloud bookkeeping and payroll for small businesses).

## Who we are

Quillbook is cloud bookkeeping and payroll for small businesses: invoices, bank reconciliation, payroll and direct deposit, year-end tax forms, reports. Founded in Denver in 2015. 42,000 business customers. 3,100 partner accountants and bookkeepers look after about 60% of those customers and often choose the software their clients use. 520 staff.

## A letter from the CEO, Sarah Kim

> Our customers do not love bookkeeping software; they love not having to think about it. On January 1 several things change at once: the IRS's new filing system becomes the only way to file the 1099 forms our customers send, many states raise their minimum wage, and payroll tax tables reset. Every provider has to switch, and the last time the sector went through a change like this, filings and payrolls were slow for days. It lands in the busiest weeks of the year for accountants, and a third of our annual plans renew in January, with a price increase we need. If accountants lose faith in us now, they will move their clients in February.

## Where things stand on November 2

- 42,000 customers: 55% micro (1–9 staff), 35% small (10–49), 10% medium (50–250).
- Plans: Starter $40 a month · Pro $90 · Plus $180. About half pay annually.
- About 4,000 annual plans renew in weeks 9 and 10, with a planned 20% price increase.
- 3,100 partner accountants and bookkeepers. The largest 600 look after about two thirds of the customers that partners manage.
- 18,000 customers run payroll with Quillbook.
- Monthly churn 1.6%. Support answers in 3 hours on average, 8 hours at year-end.
- Data export is always free.

## Our customers

## What the board wants by January 10

1. At least 92% of the annual plans due in weeks 9 and 10 renew.
2. Monthly churn no higher than 2%.
3. Partner accountants at least as satisfied as in November.
4. Support answers within 4 hours, outside January 4 and 5.
5. Both decisions together within $1.5 million.

## Red lines

- No false or misleading statement, and no blaming a government agency or a partner without evidence.
- Data export stays free; no lock-in.
- Legal deadlines cannot move.
- Prices only at the levels in the menu.

## Business as usual (what happens if you do nothing)

- The year-end update ships on December 28; it switches everything on January 1.
- Support by ticket and phone.
- Renewal emails go out 30 days before, with the new price.
- Engineers update the status page during an incident.

## What you know is coming

**Week 9, Friday, January 1: the year-end switch.**
- From January 1, 1099 forms can only be filed through the IRS's new system.
- New state minimum wages and payroll tax tables take effect for the first payrolls of the year.
- Quillbook's update is built; testing is 80% done.
- At the last big change, providers across the sector had slowdowns in the first working days.
- It is the busiest time of year for accountants: year-end closing, first payrolls, tax forms.
- About 4,000 annual plans renew in weeks 9 and 10.
