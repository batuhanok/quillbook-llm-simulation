Run types explained


Saved actual plan: customer state through the latest simulated week, using our locked decisions and released announcements.


Decision comparisons: tests of alternative packages. These are experiments, not additional company decisions.


No-action baseline: normal company operations and events, without our intervention packages.


Earlier rehearsal: practice runs with older inputs, kept as historical evidence.


Final five: plan_1 / plan_2 use our locked plan with two seeds; nodecision_1 / nodecision_2 use no interventions with those seeds; plan_nosurprises uses our plan without surprise events or their updates. The known January change stays in all five.


A seed is a different model sample, not a different customer base. Matched runs use the same 24 base profiles and keep their histories separate.




Targets: renewal ≥92% · churn ≤2% · accountant satisfaction change ≥0 · support ≤4h. Short tests cannot measure every goal. Results are synthetic, with churn/support proxies.
