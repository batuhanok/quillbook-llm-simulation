# Quillbook: board memo
**Team 08 · Ten-week simulation ending January 10**

## Decisions and investment

Quillbook committed **$1.38M** of its $1.5M budget to two packages. Week 5 combined a 10% renewal increase instead of 20%, 40 additional support staff for six weeks, and outside testing ($950,000). Week 8 added an accountant hotline, incident reports and text alerts, and a six-month commission increase to 20% ($430,000). Both packages targeted everyone; their locked wording and effective weeks were preserved.

## Results

| Scenario | Renewal % | Four-week churn %¹ | Accountant change² | Support hours³ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Plan, seed 42 | 86.00 | 0.329 | +0.516 | 5.67 |
| Plan, seed 43 | 87.88 | 0.074 | +0.548 | 5.66 |
| No decisions, seed 42 | 83.25 | 0.210 | +0.210 | 8.00 |
| No decisions, seed 43 | 83.50 | 0.062 | +0.000 | 8.06 |
| Plan, no surprises | 86.44 | 0.450 | +0.645 | 5.79 |

¹ Highest rolling four-week departure rate, a monthly proxy. ² Weighted final minus starting satisfaction on a 1–5 scale. ³ Maximum daily weighted response time, excluding January 4–5.

The plan produces **110–175 additional renewals** among 4,000 due plans and reduces modeled support waits by approximately 2.3–2.4 hours against the same-seed alternatives. Accountant sentiment improves. Churn is slightly higher with the plan, although below the 2% limit. **Renewal remains below 92%, and support exceeds four hours.**

## Interpretation and next priorities

The investment improves several outcomes but does not establish an adequate financial return. These are synthetic cohort responses across two seeds. Support results depend strongly on the assumed 100-person baseline team: the staffing/testing formula has a roughly 5.36-hour year-end floor before hotline tradeoffs. Testing benefits beyond support workload and commission benefits after January 10 are not quantified. The no-surprises comparison retains incident references in the required unchanged company messages.

Management should monitor completed renewals, accountant recommendations versus actual client departures, and support waits by customer group. Capacity validation and the longer-term value of testing and partner incentives are the next evidence priorities.
